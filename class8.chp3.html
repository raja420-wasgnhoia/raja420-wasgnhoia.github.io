FORMULAS / RULES / STEPS

1. Square of a Number

Square of a number = Number × Number
n² = n × n
(a × b)² = a² × b²

2. Square Root

√a = b, if b² = a
√(a × b) = √a × √b
√(a / b) = √a / √b, where b is positive

3. Square Root by Prime Factorization

Step 1: Express the number as a product of prime factors.
Step 2: Make pairs of identical prime factors.
Step 3: Take one factor from each pair and multiply them.

4. Cube of a Number

Cube of a number = Number × Number × Number
n³ = n × n × n
(a × b)³ = a³ × b³

5. Cube Root

∛a = b, if b³ = a
∛(a × b) = ∛a × ∛b

6. Cube Root by Prime Factorization

Step 1: Express the number as a product of prime factors.
Step 2: Make groups of three identical prime factors.
Step 3: Take one factor from each group and multiply them.
For a perfect cube, every prime factor must occur in groups of three.

CONCEPTS

Perfect Squares

A number is a perfect square if it is the square of an integer.

Example: 25 = 5²

Non-Perfect Squares

A number that is not the square of an integer is called a non-perfect square.

Perfect Cubes

A number is a perfect cube if it is the cube of an integer.

Example: 27 = 3³

Non-Perfect Cubes

A number that is not the cube of an integer is called a non-perfect cube.

Prime Factorization

Prime factorization expresses a number as a product of prime numbers.

Pairs are used to find square roots, while groups of three are used to find cube roots.

DEFINITIONS

1. Square

The product obtained by multiplying a number by itself is called its square.

2. Square Root

A number which, when multiplied by itself, gives the original number is called its square root.

3. Perfect Square

A number that can be expressed as the square of an integer is called a perfect square.

4. Cube

The product obtained by multiplying a number by itself three times is called its cube.

5. Perfect Cube

A number that can be expressed as the cube of an integer is called a perfect cube.

6. Cube Root

A number which, when multiplied by itself three times, gives the original number is called its cube root.

KEY POINTS

  • The square of a number is obtained by multiplying it by itself.
  • The cube of a number is obtained by multiplying it by itself three times.
  • The square root is the inverse operation of squaring.
  • The cube root is the inverse operation of cubing.
  • Perfect squares have prime factors in pairs.
  • Perfect cubes have prime factors in groups of three.
  • Every positive perfect square has a whole-number square root.
  • Every perfect cube has an integer cube root when its cube root is an integer.
