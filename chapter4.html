📌 Key Points

• Finite Sequence: A sequence with a countable number of terms and a last term.

• Infinite Sequence: A sequence that continues without bound.

• A formula for the nth term is called the general term.

• Σk=1n [kᵐ − (k − 1)ᵐ] = nᵐ

• Σk=1n 1 = n

• Σk=1n k = n(n + 1)/2
