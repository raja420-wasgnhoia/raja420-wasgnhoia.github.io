📌 Key Points

• Fundamental law is the basic law from which other trigonometric formulas are derived.

• Allied angle rules tell when trigonometric ratios change and when they do not change, and how to decide their signs.

• Double angle formulas are used to find trigonometric functions of double angles from single-angle formulas.

• Half angle formulas are used to find trigonometric functions of half angles from full-angle formulas.

• Triple angle formula is used to find the sine or cosine of three times an angle.

• An expression containing both sine and cosine of the same angle can be converted into a single sine or cosine function.
