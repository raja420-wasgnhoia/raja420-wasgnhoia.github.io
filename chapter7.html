📌 Key Points

• Mathematical induction has two main steps: Base Case and Inductive Step.

• In the base case, we prove the formula for n = 1.

• In the inductive step, assume the formula is true for n = k and prove it for n = k + 1.

• If both steps are proved, the formula is true for all natural numbers.

• Pascal's Triangle gives the coefficients of the binomial expansion.

• Each number in Pascal's Triangle is the sum of the two numbers directly above it.

• In the expansion of (a + b)n, the total number of terms is n + 1.

• The sum of the powers of a and b in every term is n.

• The coefficients from the beginning and the end are equal.

• For finding remainder, convert the number into (a + 1)n or (a − 1)n form.

• For finding unit or last digits, use the binomial theorem to separate large powers into multiples of 10 or 100.
