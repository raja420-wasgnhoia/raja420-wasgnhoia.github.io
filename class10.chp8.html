FORMULAS

Basic Trigonometric Ratios

sin θ = Perpendicular / Hypotenuse = a/c
cos θ = Base / Hypotenuse = b/c
tan θ = Perpendicular / Base = a/b
cosec θ = 1/sin θ = c/a
sec θ = 1/cos θ = c/b
cot θ = 1/tan θ = b/a

Unit Circle

cos θ = x
sin θ = y
tan θ = y/x
cot θ = x/y
sec θ = 1/x
cosec θ = 1/y

Angles Between 90° and 180°

cos(180° − θ) = −cos θ
sin(180° − θ) = sin θ
tan(180° − θ) = −tan θ
cot(180° − θ) = −cot θ
sec(180° − θ) = −sec θ
cosec(180° − θ) = cosec θ

Right Triangle

a² + b² = c²
α + β = 90°
tan β = b/a
b = a tan β
c = √(a² + b²)

Elevation and Depression

tan θ = h/x
x = h/tan θ

Law of Cosines

a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos α
b² = a² + c² − 2ac cos β
c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos γ
cos α = (b² + c² − a²)/(2bc)
cos β = (a² + c² − b²)/(2ac)
cos γ = (a² + b² − c²)/(2ab)

Law of Sines

a/sin α = b/sin β = c/sin γ
sin α/a = sin β/b = sin γ/c
α + β + γ = 180°
h = b sin α

Law of Tangents

(a+b)/(a−b) = tan[(α+β)/2] / tan[(α−β)/2]
(b+c)/(b−c) = tan[(β+γ)/2] / tan[(β−γ)/2]
(c+a)/(c−a) = tan[(γ+α)/2] / tan[(γ−α)/2]

Half-Angle Formulas

S = (a+b+c)/2
cos(α/2) = √[S(S−a)/(bc)]
cos(β/2) = √[S(S−b)/(ac)]
cos(γ/2) = √[S(S−c)/(ab)]
sin(α/2) = √[(S−b)(S−c)/(bc)]
sin(β/2) = √[(S−a)(S−c)/(ac)]
sin(γ/2) = √[(S−a)(S−b)/(ab)]
tan(α/2) = √[(S−b)(S−c)/(S(S−a))]
tan(β/2) = √[(S−a)(S−c)/(S(S−b))]
tan(γ/2) = √[(S−a)(S−b)/(S(S−c))]

Area of Triangle

Δ = 1/2 × base × height
Δ = 1/2 bc sin α
Δ = 1/2 ac sin β
Δ = 1/2 ab sin γ
Δ = 1/2 a²(sin β sin γ / sin α)
Δ = 1/2 b²(sin α sin γ / sin β)
Δ = 1/2 c²(sin α sin β / sin γ)

Heron's Formula

S = (a+b+c)/2
Δ = √[S(S−a)(S−b)(S−c)]

Circumradius

R = a/(2 sin α)
R = b/(2 sin β)
R = c/(2 sin γ)
R = abc/(4Δ)
Diameter = 2R

Inradius

r = Δ/S
Δ = rS
Δ = 1/2 r(a+b+c)

CONCEPTS

Quadrant Signs

Quadrant I: All ratios are positive.
Quadrant II: Sine and cosec are positive.
Quadrant III: Tangent and cot are positive.
Quadrant IV: Cosine and sec are positive.

Reference Angle

The trigonometric ratio has the same absolute value as its reference angle. Its sign is determined by the quadrant.

Law Selection

Law of Cosines: Mainly used for SAS and SSS cases.
Law of Sines: Mainly used for ASA, AAS and appropriate SSA cases.
Law of Tangents: Used for solving oblique triangles in situations similar to the Law of Cosines.

Ambiguous Case

In the SSA case, there may be zero, one or two possible triangles. The number of solutions depends on the given side, angle and height.

Area Selection

Use 1/2 ab sin γ when two sides and their included angle are known.
Use the AAS/ASA area formula when two angles and one side are known.
Use Heron's Formula when all three sides are known.

Important Relationship

Pythagoras' Theorem is a special case of the Law of Cosines when the included angle is 90°.

DEFINITIONS

Quadrantal Angle: An angle whose terminal ray coincides with an axis in standard position.
Coterminal Angles: Angles having the same terminal ray in standard position.
Trigonometric Ratio: The ratio between any two sides of a right-angled triangle.
Oblique Triangle: A triangle that is not a right triangle. It may be acute-angled or obtuse-angled.
Angle of Elevation: The angle between the horizontal line and the line of sight when an object is viewed upward.
Angle of Depression: The angle between the horizontal line and the line of sight when an object is viewed downward.
Circumcircle: A circle passing through all vertices of a triangle.
Circumcentre: The point of intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of the sides of a triangle.
Circumradius: The radius of the circumcircle, denoted by R.
Incircle: A circle drawn inside a triangle and touching all three sides.
Incentre: The point of intersection of the angle bisectors of a triangle.
Inradius: The radius of the incircle, denoted by r.
Escribed Circle: A circle touching one side of a triangle externally and the extensions of its other two sides.
E-centre: The intersection of one internal angle bisector and the external angle bisectors of the remaining two angles.
Heron's Formula: A formula used to find the area of a triangle when all three sides are known.

KEY POINTS

  • For every triangle, α + β + γ = 180°.
  • ASTC is used to determine the signs of trigonometric ratios in different quadrants.
  • For an angle in the second quadrant, the reference angle is 180° − θ.
  • Use the Law of Cosines for SAS and SSS situations.
  • Use the Law of Sines for ASA, AAS and suitable SSA situations.
  • In the SSA ambiguous case, there can be 0, 1 or 2 triangles.
  • S = (a+b+c)/2 is the semiperimeter.
  • Heron's Formula is used when three sides are given.
  • The area formula Δ = 1/2 ab sin γ requires two sides and their included angle.
  • R = abc/(4Δ) gives the circumradius.
  • r = Δ/S gives the inradius.
  • The angle of depression equals the corresponding angle of elevation.
  • Pythagoras' Theorem is a special case of the Law of Cosines.
