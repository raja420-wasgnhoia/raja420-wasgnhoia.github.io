Formulas

sin θ = y/r
cos θ = x/r
−1 ≤ sin θ ≤ 1
−1 ≤ cos θ ≤ 1
|sec θ| ≥ 1
|csc θ| ≥ 1
csc θ = 1/sin θ
sec θ = 1/cos θ
f(x+p) = f(x)
If period of f(x) is p, then period of f(ax+b) is p/|a|
Period of sin x = 2π
Period of cos x = 2π
Period of tan x = π
Period of cot x = π
Period of sec x = 2π
Period of csc x = 2π
Maximum of a + b sin θ = a + |b|
Minimum of a + b sin θ = a − |b|
Maximum of a + b cos θ = a + |b|
Minimum of a + b cos θ = a − |b|
Maximum of a + b sin(cθ+d) = a + |b|
Minimum of a + b sin(cθ+d) = a − |b|
Maximum of a + b cos(cθ+d) = a + |b|
Minimum of a + b cos(cθ+d) = a − |b|
For y = a + b sin(cθ+d): Amplitude = |b|
For y = a + b cos(cθ+d): Amplitude = |b|
Period of sin(bx) = 2π/|b|
Period of cos(bx) = 2π/|b|
Period of tan(bx) = π/|b|
Period of cot(bx) = π/|b|
If maximum = M > 0 and minimum = m > 0, then for the reciprocal function: Maximum = 1/m
If maximum = M > 0 and minimum = m > 0, then for the reciprocal function: Minimum = 1/M
f(−x) = −f(x) → Odd Function
f(−x) = f(x) → Even Function

Graph Characteristics

y = sin x: Range = [−1,1], Period = 2π, Amplitude = 1, Odd
y = cos x: Range = [−1,1], Period = 2π, Amplitude = 1, Even
y = tan x: Range = R, Period = π, Amplitude = none, Odd
y = csc x: Range = (−∞,−1] ∪ [1,∞), Period = 2π, Odd
y = sec x: Range = (−∞,−1] ∪ [1,∞), Period = 2π, Even
y = cot x: Range = R, Period = π, Amplitude = none, Odd
y = 3sin 2x: Range = [−3,3], Period = π, Amplitude = 3, Odd
y = 3cos 2x: Range = [−3,3], Period = π, Amplitude = 3, Even

📌 Key Points

• Domain means all x-values for which a function is defined.

• Range means all y-values which a function can take.

• Sin and cos are defined for all real numbers and their values lie between −1 and 1.

• Tan is not defined at odd multiples of π/2.

• Cot is not defined at multiples of π.

• Sec is not defined at odd multiples of π/2.

• Cosec is not defined at multiples of π.

• A periodic function repeats its values after a fixed interval.

• Sin, cos, sec and cosec have period 2π.

• Tan and cot have period π.

• Multiplying a function by a constant or adding a constant does not change its period.

• sin x, tan x, cot x and cosec x are odd functions.

• cos x and sec x are even functions.

• The graph of an even function is symmetric about the y-axis.

• The graph of an odd function is symmetric about the origin.

• In y = a sin(bx) or y = a cos(bx), |a| represents the amplitude.

• For y = a sin(bx) or y = a cos(bx), the period is 2π/|b|.

• For y = a tan(bx) or y = a cot(bx), the period is π/|b|.

• Cosec and sec graphs have values only outside the interval (−1,1).

• Tan, cot and their graphs have no amplitude.

• In y = a + b sin(cθ+d) or y = a + b cos(cθ+d), a represents the vertical shift and |b| represents amplitude.
