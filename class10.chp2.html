Important Formulas

Quadratic Equation: ax² + bx + c = 0, a ≠ 0
Zero Product Property: If ab = 0, then a = 0 or b = 0.
Quadratic Formula: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a
Discriminant: D = b² − 4ac
Sum of Roots: S₁ + S₂ = −b/a
Product of Roots: S₁S₂ = c/a
Equation from Given Roots: x² − (S₁ + S₂)x + S₁S₂ = 0
Using Sum S and Product P: x² − Sx + P = 0
Completing Square: x² + bx = (x + b/2)² − (b/2)²
Difference of Squares: a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
Type 1 Substitution: x² = y, therefore x⁴ = y²
Type 2 Substitution: P(x) = y
Reciprocal Substitution: y = x + 1/x
Reciprocal Identity: x² + 1/x² = y² − 2
Exponential Substitution: kˣ = y
Volume of Cuboid: V = lwh

Nature of Roots

D > 0: Two real and unequal roots.
D = 0: Two real and equal roots.
D < 0: Two imaginary/complex roots.
Positive perfect-square D: Real, rational and unequal roots.
Positive non-square D: Real, irrational and unequal roots.

Definitions

  • Quadratic Equation: An equation of degree 2 in one variable, written as ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0.
  • Standard Form: A quadratic equation written with all terms on the left side and zero on the right side.
  • Root: A value of the variable that makes the equation true.
  • Discriminant: The expression b² − 4ac that determines the nature of the roots.
  • Reciprocal Equation: An equation that remains unchanged when the variable is replaced by its reciprocal.
  • Exponential Equation: An equation in which the variable occurs in an exponent, such as kˣ.

Key Points

  • Always convert a quadratic equation into standard form before solving.
  • In factorization, use the zero product property.
  • While completing the square, add the same quantity to both sides.
  • For ax² + bx + c = 0, identify a, b and c carefully.
  • Negative or impossible physical dimensions are rejected.
  • For x² = y substitution, solve for y first and then substitute back.
  • For reciprocal equations, x ≠ 0.
  • For exponential equations, kˣ is always positive when k > 0.
  • When roots are given, use: x² − (sum of roots)x + product of roots = 0.
  • Roots 3 + 2i and 3 − 2i give: x² − 6x + 13 = 0.
