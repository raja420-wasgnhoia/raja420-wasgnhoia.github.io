FORMULAS & RULES

1. Cost Price, Selling Price, Profit and Loss

Cost Price (C.P.) = Price at which an article is purchased.
Selling Price (S.P.) = Price at which an article is sold.
If S.P. > C.P., there is a Profit.
If C.P. > S.P., there is a Loss.
Profit = S.P. − C.P.
Loss = C.P. − S.P.
Profit % = (Profit ÷ C.P.) × 100
Loss % = (Loss ÷ C.P.) × 100
Profit and loss percentages are calculated on Cost Price.

2. Discount

Marked Price (M.P.) = Price written or displayed on an article.
Discount = M.P. − S.P.
Discount % = (Discount ÷ M.P.) × 100
S.P. after discount = M.P. − Discount
Discount percentage is calculated on the Marked Price.

3. Property Tax

Property Tax = (Property Value × Tax Rate) ÷ 100
Property Value = (Property Tax × 100) ÷ Tax Rate

4. General Sales Tax (GST)

GST Amount = (Original Price × GST Rate) ÷ 100
Price Including GST = Original Price + GST Amount
Price Including GST = Original Price × (1 + Rate/100)
Original Price = (Price Including GST × 100) ÷ (100 + Rate)

5. Commission

Commission = (Base Amount × Commission Rate) ÷ 100
Net Amount after Commission = Base Amount − Commission
Total Pay = Salary + Commission

6. Value Added Tax (VAT)

Input Tax = Tax paid on purchases
Output Tax = Tax collected on sales
VAT Payable = Output Tax − Input Tax
Tax Amount = (Price × Tax Rate) ÷ 100
Taxable Purchase Price = Original Purchase Price + Input Tax

7. Zakat

Rate of Zakat = 2.5% = 1/40
Zakat = Total Eligible Amount × 2.5/100
Total Eligible Amount = Zakat × 100 ÷ 2.5
Gold Nisab = 7.5 tolas ≈ 87.4 grams
Silver Nisab = 52.5 tolas ≈ 612.32 grams
1 Tola ≈ 11.65 grams
Zakat is generally due on eligible wealth meeting Nisab and held for one complete lunar year, subject to applicable rules.

8. Ushr

Ushr on Naturally Irrigated Crops = Crop Produce × 10/100
Ushr on Artificially Irrigated Crops = Crop Produce × 5/100
Natural Irrigation: Rain, rivers, streams and natural water sources.
Artificial Irrigation: Wells and artificially supplied water.

9. Income Tax

Annual Income = Monthly Income × 12
Net Income after Zakat = Annual Income − Applicable Zakat
Taxable Income = Net Income − Applicable Rebate
Income Tax = Taxable Income × Tax Rate/100
Tax after Reduction = Calculated Tax − Tax Reduction
Tax Reduction = Calculated Tax × Reduction Rate/100
Total Income = Income from Property + Business Income + Salary + Other Income

CONCEPTS

1. Profit and Loss

Profit occurs when an article is sold for more than its cost price. Loss occurs when it is sold for less than its cost price.

2. Discount

Discount is the reduction offered on the marked price of an article. Its percentage is calculated using the marked price, not the selling price.

3. Direct and Indirect Taxes

Direct Tax: A tax imposed directly on income or wealth, such as income tax.

Indirect Tax: A tax imposed on goods, services or expenditure, such as sales tax.

4. GST

General Sales Tax is charged on certain goods and services. The amount depends on the applicable tax rate and the price of the item.

5. Commission

Commission is an amount calculated as a percentage of sales, property price or another agreed base amount.

6. VAT

Value Added Tax is collected at different stages of production and distribution. A business generally pays the difference between output tax and eligible input tax.

Sales tax, as described in the textbook comparison, is collected at the final point of purchase, while VAT is collected across multiple stages.

7. Zakat

Zakat is an obligatory form of giving in Islam. For eligible wealth, the commonly taught rate is 2.5%, subject to Nisab and other applicable conditions.

8. Nisab

Nisab is the minimum threshold of eligible wealth at which Zakat becomes due. The textbook gives gold and silver thresholds; the applicable calculation can depend on the relevant scholarly guidance.

9. Lunar Year

A lunar year is based on the cycles of the Moon and is shorter than a solar year.

10. Ushr

Ushr is Zakat on agricultural produce and is paid at harvest. The textbook gives a rate of 10% for natural irrigation and 5% for artificial irrigation.

11. Income Tax

Income tax is a direct tax imposed on personal income according to the applicable tax rules and thresholds.

12. Rebate

A rebate is an amount or allowance that reduces the income subject to tax under the rules used in the textbook.

13. Financial Year

The textbook describes a financial year running from 1 July to 30 June.

DEFINITIONS

Cost Price (C.P.): The price at which an article is purchased.

Selling Price (S.P.): The price at which an article is sold.

Profit: The amount by which the selling price exceeds the cost price.

Loss: The amount by which the cost price exceeds the selling price.

Marked Price: The price written or displayed on an article.

Discount: The reduction in the marked price of an article.

Property Tax: A tax imposed on property according to applicable rules.

General Sales Tax (GST): A tax charged on certain goods and services.

Commission: Payment calculated as a percentage of a specified amount, such as sales.

Input Tax: Tax paid by a business on its purchases.

Output Tax: Tax charged or collected by a business on its sales.

Value Added Tax (VAT): A tax on value added at stages of production and distribution.

Zakat: A specific obligatory contribution from eligible wealth given to eligible recipients under Islamic rules.

Nisab: The minimum threshold of wealth at which Zakat becomes due.

Ushr: Zakat on agricultural produce, paid at harvest.

Income Tax: A tax levied directly on personal income.

Rebate: An allowance or amount that reduces taxable income or tax according to the applicable rules.

Lunar Year: A year based on the cycles of the Moon.

KEY POINTS

  • Profit = S.P. − C.P.; Loss = C.P. − S.P.
  • Profit and loss percentages are calculated on cost price.
  • Discount percentage is calculated on marked price.
  • Tax amounts are calculated using the applicable rate and taxable base.
  • Property tax rates can vary according to the relevant rules and type of property.
  • GST rates may vary by goods, services and tax period. Do not assume one rate applies to everything.
  • VAT payable = Output Tax − Input Tax.
  • Sales tax and VAT are related but may be collected differently.
  • Zakat is commonly taught at 2.5% of eligible wealth, subject to Nisab and applicable conditions.
  • The textbook gives gold Nisab as approximately 87.4 grams and silver Nisab as approximately 612.32 grams.
  • Ushr is paid on agricultural produce at harvest.
  • The textbook gives Ushr as 10% for natural irrigation and 5% for artificial irrigation.
  • Annual income = Monthly income × 12.
  • Taxable income and tax reductions must be calculated according to the rules given in the question.
  • Tax rates, rebates and allowances in textbook examples may not represent current legal rates.
